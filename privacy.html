Privacy Policy

LEDGER — RETIREMENT CALCULATOR · EFFECTIVE 1 SEPTEMBER 2026

Ledger is a retirement calculator. It keeps what you enter on your device and asks for nothing about you — no account, no sign-in, no name, no email, no advertising identifier. This policy says plainly what the app holds, what it sends, and where the one thing it does send goes.

What stays on your device

Everything you type — your age, salary, balance, contribution rate, and every other input — is stored on your device alone, in the app's own storage. So is The Register, the dated history of scenarios you save. None of it is uploaded, backed up to us, or readable by us. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What is sent, and when

Three features are written by AI: the margin note on the index, the reading under a saved comparison, and the console you open from the machine square. All three run only while a Ledger AI subscription is on. The console sends only when you ask it a question; the margin note and the reading are requested automatically when their screens open, so that the note is already there when you look.

Your figures live on your device. They leave it only while Ledger AI is on: where your device can write these features itself, it does, and nothing leaves. Where it cannot, the app sends the worksheet's printed figures and labels — the same lines you can already see on screen — to a proxy service we operate, which passes them to the AI model that writes the prose. Nothing else is sent. No name, no account, no device identifier, no contact details, no location, no contents of The Register beyond the comparison you are reading.

  • Our proxy runs on Cloudflare Workers. It stores nothing — no database, no logs of your figures. It holds the request only long enough to answer it.
  • Cloudflare handles the network request and sees the originating IP address, which is used to rate-limit the service and is not retained by us or joined to anything.
  • Each request also carries your Ledger AI subscription's own App Store receipt, so the service can confirm the subscription is live before answering. It identifies the subscription, not you — no name, no email, no payment details — and it is kept only briefly, to avoid re-checking on every request.
  • The prose is written by a model served by Together AI, which processes the request under its own privacy terms.

Without a Ledger AI subscription, or with those features unused, the app sends nothing at all.

Dictation

The console's microphone key transcribes speech into the input line using Apple's speech recognition, preferring the on-device recogniser where your device supports it. The microphone is live only while you hold that key on. Ledger keeps no recording and transmits no audio.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details, card number, or Apple Account. The app asks Apple whether a purchase is present, and the one place a purchase record travels is the AI service above: requests carry the subscription's own receipt so the service can confirm it is live.

What we do not do

  • No tracking, and no data is shared with data brokers or advertisers.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service. The app contains no third-party SDK of any kind.
  • No selling of personal information, and no sharing of it for cross-context advertising.
  • No profiles built about you. There is no account to build one against.

Children

Ledger is a financial calculator for adults and is not directed to children under 13. It knowingly collects nothing from them.

Your rights

Because the app holds no personal information about you on any server, there is no account to access, correct, export, or delete. The data is yours, on your device, and removing the app removes it. If you have a question about this, write to us.

Not advice

Ledger shows arithmetic, not recommendations. Every figure is a projection built on the assumptions you enter. Nothing in the app is investment, tax, or legal advice.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and the revised policy is posted at this address before it takes effect.
