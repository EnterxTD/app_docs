Privacy Policy

LEDGER · UPDATED 13 OCTOBER 2026

Ledger is a retirement calculator with optional AI features. You enter your own numbers; Ledger does not connect to your bank or brokerage, and no Ledger account is required. This policy describes the data stored in the app and the data used by its optional services.

Inputs and saved snapshots

Your inputs, dated progress updates, and Register snapshots are stored in the app's storage on your device. Ledger does not provide a server account that syncs this record. You can preview and share a PDF report of the Register using the export action, including saved assumptions, results, and recorded progress. Copies you save, print, or share are handled by the destinations you choose. The app does not upload the whole Register for AI processing; only the figures relevant to a feature are sent as described below.

Deleting the app removes its local data. Copies you exported or included in device backups may remain under the controls of the apps or backup services you used.

AI features and what they send

The calculators run locally and do not need AI processing. All tools require an active Ledger trial or subscription. Disabling AI sharing does not disable calculators for subscribers. Cloud processing requires your permission as well as an active subscription. Ask Ledger requests permission when opened; you can withdraw it in Memory & privacy. After you allow cloud processing:

  • Comparison readings use Apple's on-device model when available. Otherwise, opening or changing a comparison can send its displayed labels and before-and-after figures to cloud AI.
  • The assistant generates conversational answers and suggested questions through Together AI via Cloudflare. Opening Ask Ledger, changing its context or inputs, or continuing a conversation can automatically request suggested questions after consent. These requests send the selected context's facts and assumptions and bounded recent conversation turns. Answer requests also include your question. The selected context can be a calculator, saved-plan comparison, or progress review. Saved preferences are included only if you turn on their separate sharing setting. Text you include in a question is part of that request. Ledger calculates proposed results locally and requires your tap to apply a change.
  • Progress reviews can select highlights when you open a report, using Apple's on-device model when available. Otherwise, cloud AI receives a small set of observations containing recorded balances, dates, employee contributions, and changes computed from those records. The review request excludes projected balances, assumed future contributions, saved alternatives, and chat history. AI selects existing observations; it cannot change their figures or apply changes to your plan.

Cloud requests go to a service we operate on Cloudflare Workers, then to a model hosted by Together AI. Each request also carries the signed App Store transaction for your Ledger AI subscription to check access. It contains purchase identifiers and subscription details, not your payment-card details or Apple Account password. Requests can therefore be associated with the same subscription even though Ledger does not ask for your name or email.

Service data and retention

Conversation history, drafts, and preferences you explicitly save are stored on your device. You can search or delete conversations, turn off conversation-history retention, and inspect, edit, or delete saved preferences in the assistant. Saved review summaries have separate deletion controls. Clearing conversations does not delete your financial inputs, Register snapshots, saved review summaries, or saved preferences. Deleting local history does not erase requests already processed by cloud providers.

The AI service uses subscription identifiers, cached access checks, and request timing to validate subscriptions and limit abuse. Network providers also process connection metadata, such as IP addresses, to deliver and protect the service. Service errors may be recorded for operational troubleshooting. These functions are separate from the local Register.

Together AI processes prompts and responses under its privacy policy and the account's retention settings. Its privacy documentation describes prompt and response storage by default and an organization setting for zero data retention. Ledger does not promise zero data retention for these cloud requests.

Dictation

The assistant's microphone button transcribes speech into the input line. You can review or edit the text before sending it. Ledger requests microphone and speech-recognition permission when you use this feature and does not store recordings or send audio to its AI proxy.

Speech recognition stays on-device where the recognizer supports it. Otherwise, audio may be sent to Apple for transcription. Apple's handling depends on your device and privacy settings; see Siri, Dictation & Privacy. You can change Ledger's access in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, Microphone and Speech Recognition.

Purchases and notifications

Apple handles purchases and subscriptions. Ledger reads the verified purchase status to open the tools you own; the signed subscription transaction is also used by the AI service as described above. Ledger does not receive your card information.

If you start a free trial, the app may ask permission to send a local renewal reminder. Ledger does not send marketing notifications.

Tracking and advertising

Ledger has no advertising identifier, third-party analytics SDK, or advertising SDK. Its service data is used to provide and protect the app's functions, not to track you across other companies' apps or websites or to sell information to advertisers.

Children

Ledger is intended for adults planning retirement and is not directed to children under 13.

Review reminders

Quarterly review reminders are on by default and scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off in Your progress, including before your first review. After your first review, Ledger asks for iOS notification permission if needed; alerts cannot appear without permission. You can also set a date manually. Existing opt-outs are preserved. Reminder preferences and the figures last included in a review are stored locally to avoid duplicate in-app invitations. Reading a review moves an enabled reminder three months ahead; an ignored reminder does not generate follow-up nudges. Notifications contain no financial figures and do not send your progress to a notification server or request AI processing in the background. You can turn reminders off in Your progress or change notification permission in device Settings.

Your choices and contact

Every tool needs an active Ledger trial or subscription. You choose whether to allow cloud AI, whether to send a question to Ask Ledger, and whether to use dictation; Apple manages your purchases. You can revoke cloud permission in Memory & privacy. While permission and a subscription are active, comparison readings, suggested questions, and progress-review highlights can be requested automatically as described above. Suggestions inside Ask Ledger can be dismissed or turned off in Memory & privacy.

Not advice

Ledger shows calculations based on your assumptions. Results and AI responses are estimates, not investment, tax, or legal advice.

Changes
