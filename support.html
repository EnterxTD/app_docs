LEDGER — RETIREMENT CALCULATOR

Ledger has eleven retirement calculators, the Simulation, and Ask Ledger. You can save snapshots to compare, record your progress, and export PDF reports. Enter your own numbers without connecting a bank account or creating a Ledger account.

Subscription access

Every tool requires an active trial or subscription: all eleven calculators, the Simulation, the Register, PDF export, and the Appendix. Guided onboarding can demonstrate an initial estimate before you subscribe. Cloud AI is included but remains optional and requires separate permission.

Eligible subscribers get three days free, then $14.99/year or $2.99/month (US). The selected subscription automatically renews unless canceled. Current prices and eligibility appear before purchase. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings.

Snapshots and progress

Open the Register, choose Saved snapshots, and tap Save snapshot to keep today's inputs and results with the date. Choose two snapshots to compare them side by side. Saved snapshots keep their original results.

Choose Your progress to record dated balances and employee contributions. A progress review shows the balances you recorded, kept separate from your current projection. Quarterly reminders are on by default, require iOS notification permission, and can be rescheduled or turned off in Your progress.

Use Export in the Register to preview a PDF of your saved assumptions, results, and recorded progress, then save, print, or share it. Export becomes available when the Register contains a snapshot or progress record.

Restoring a purchase

On the subscription screen, tap Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so a new device restores them at no charge. If restore reports nothing to restore while you believe you have paid, check that you are signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

Refunds and cancellation

Apple handles both. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Manage or cancel the subscription in Settings, under your Apple Account, in Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal. Access continues until expiration; then all tools close. Your inputs, saved snapshots, progress, and conversations stay on your device, and every tool reopens when you subscribe again.

Your figures

Your inputs and records are stored on your device. Ledger does not sync the Register to a server account. Deleting the app removes local data, but exports and device backups may remain.

While subscribed, Ledger AI can send relevant figures, your questions, and subscription verification data through Cloudflare and Together AI after you allow cloud processing. Comparison readings, suggested questions, and progress-review highlights can then be requested automatically. You can turn off Ledger AI in Ask Ledger › Memory & privacy. Dictation may send audio to Apple for transcription. Read the privacy policy for the feature-specific data flows and retention details.

Ask Ledger

Tap the Ask bar at the bottom of any screen to open Ask Ledger. It opens in a sheet on iPhone and beside the calculator on a wide iPad window. Answers and suggested questions come from your own retirement numbers and your conversation. Ledger AI needs an internet connection, an active subscription, and your permission to share the relevant figures with Together AI through Cloudflare. Permission is requested when you first open Ask Ledger; subscribed calculators remain available without enabling cloud AI. If generation is unavailable, Ledger shows a status rather than a scripted answer. Minimize it to return to your work; your conversation remains available.

After an answer, open References & inputs to visit its Appendix references or choose Edit an assumption. Ledger opens the existing calculator at that control; following a link does not change the value. Follow-up prompts reflect the calculator or review you are reading. On an Appendix entry, choose Explain this reference to discuss that page's printed explanation and current examples. The Appendix is included in the same subscription as every calculator.

Try “What if I retire two years earlier?” Check the changed inputs and results, then tap Use these inputs, or tap Save snapshot to keep it without changing your inputs. You can refine a preview, discard it, or undo a change you used while those inputs remain current. If your inputs change, tap Refresh preview before you use it.

Choose Use saved snapshot or Use saved review in the Ask Ledger menu to base your next question on that item's original figures and assumptions. The context label identifies the selected source. Any unfinished preview stays in its original conversation, and your unsent question remains available.

The Ask Ledger menu holds Conversation history and Memory & privacy. Saved preferences are explicit: they do not change your assumptions, and they are shared with cloud questions only when you enable that setting. Conversation history, saved preferences, and saved review summaries have separate deletion controls. Ask Ledger needs permission, an active subscription, and an available AI service. Calculators and the printed Appendix remain available without a connection.

Switching conversations cancels a pending cloud question and keeps it as a draft in the original conversation. Turning cloud permission off or losing subscription access also stops the pending request in the app and keeps your work. You can retry after restoring access. Deleting a conversation removes its retry state as well.

Why the figures are estimates

Every calculator projects forward from assumptions you set — a rate of return, a raise, an inflation figure. Those are guesses about the future; each result carries an ESTIMATE stamp, and Terms & notes explains what that means. Ledger shows estimates, not recommendations. For which funds to hold or how to invest, talk with a qualified financial advisor.
