LEDGER — RETIREMENT CALCULATOR

Ledger is eleven retirement calculators over one shared set of figures, printed as an accounting document. Every number it shows is an estimate, not a guarantee, and nothing in the app is advice.

Contact

Write to tony.vi.dinh@gmail.com. One person reads it. Say which calculator and what you entered, and you will get a straight answer.

What is free

The retirement projection, the purchasing power calculator, and the whole appendix are free and stay free. The IRS contribution and distribution limits are updated every year for everyone, paid or not, because the arithmetic is not the thing being sold.

Ledger Unlock is one payment that opens the other nine calculators, the Monte Carlo simulation, and The Register — the dated history of your plan, where you save a scenario and compare any two side by side. Ledger AI is a yearly subscription that opens everything Ledger Unlock opens while it runs, plus the AI itself, which answers questions about your own figures and can propose changes to your inputs for you to accept or ignore. Either one opens the paid tools; the subscription is a complete alternative, not an add-on.

Restoring a purchase

Open the price list from any locked calculator and tap RESTORE. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so a new device restores them at no charge. If restore reports nothing to restore while you believe you have paid, check that you are signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

Refunds and cancellation

Apple handles both. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. Manage or cancel the subscription in Settings, under your Apple Account, in Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the one-time unlock is permanent and is not a subscription.

Your figures

Everything you enter stays on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, so there is nothing for us to delete on request — removing the app removes the data. The three AI features send only the worksheet's printed figures and labels, and only while a Ledger AI subscription is on. The privacy policy says exactly what goes where and when.

Why the figures are estimates

Every calculator projects forward from assumptions you set — a rate of return, a raise, an inflation figure. Those are guesses about the future, and the app says so on every screen. Ledger shows arithmetic, not recommendations, and it is not a substitute for a licensed adviser.
